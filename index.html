Walk into 3D worlds, right in your browser.

Got a link to a world? Click it and you're in, on a phone, a laptop or a headset, with nothing to install. WebVerse is the free, open page that opens it.

You've been sent a world. Here's how to walk around in it.

Press H in any world to see these again.

On a phone or tablet

The left stick walks, the right stick looks. Jump, Chat and Fly sit above them.

Look

Click in the world to look around with the mouse. Esc gives the mouse back.

Move

W A S D to move, Space to jump. F to fly where the world allows it: Space up, Shift down.

Picture to come Two visitors in one world with their name labels, a phone and a laptop side by side.

Whoever has the link is there with you

You see each other move, and you can talk. A door in one world can open onto another, the way one web page links to the next; step through and the address changes.

A world's scripts can do things inside the world and cannot reach your device or your data.

What it does

Opens any world from its link

and follows doors from one world into another.

Puts visitors in the same room

Others appear, and move, as they move.

Keeps a world's scripts in the world

They cannot reach your device or your data.

Shows something walkable first

and fills in the detail while you are already there.

Host your own world? Link to it like this.

A world is a folder of files at a web address. Paste the address of its index.veml and the link below opens WebVerse already pointed at it. The host has to allow cross-origin reads (Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *): GitHub Pages does; a bucket needs a CORS rule.

The link that opens it:

https://worlds.worldhub.me/webverse/index.html#world-url=…

Give visitors one link

It is WebVerse's address with the world's address after #world-url=, URL-encoded:

<a href="https://worlds.worldhub.me/webverse/index.html
   #world-url=https%3A%2F%2Fyour.site%2Froom%2Findex.veml">
  Walk in
</a>

A world published from Blender already comes with such a link.

Read the world you are in

Add /index.veml to any published world's address and the whole file opens as text. It is short, and every line means something you can see.
